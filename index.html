Gulab Jamun

Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Bread
  • Oil

Recipee

  1. Mix khoya, flour, and baking soda; knead into a soft dough.
  2. Shape small balls and deep fry on low heat until golden brown.
  3. Prepare sugar syrup with sugar, water, and cardamom.
  4. Soak fried balls in warm syrup for 1–2 hours before serving.

Barfi

Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Bread
  • Oil

Recipee

  1. Heat milk or khoya with sugar in a pan.
  2. Stir continuously until thick and semi-solid.
  3. Add cardamom and nuts for flavor.
  4. Spread on a tray, cool, and cut into square or diamond shapes.
